    How to read this dashboard

    Achievement vs Budget
    Actual revenue (01–22 Nov) ÷ full-month November budget. This is the primary lens of this dashboard. Because only 22 of 30 days have elapsed, a 73.3% reading at month end would equal budget.
    Achievement vs Forecast
    The percentage printed on the source Excel (Actual ÷ Revised Forecast). Useful for judging execution against the committed plan, but it flatters any SBU whose forecast was cut.
    Gap & run-rate
    Required daily run-rate = (Budget − Actual) ÷ 8 remaining days, compared with the run-rate achieved so far. Anything above 1.5× is arithmetically out of reach.
